Robinhood — Core Features

Connect your trades → see and evaluate them in the app · 2026-09-09

Your Robinhood account is connected. These are the features we're building on top of it — what you'll actually be able to do in the app.

What we're adding

1 Import your trades on demand building

Pull your Robinhood fills into the app whenever you want — pick a date (today, yesterday, any day) and hit a button. No waiting for a scheduled time, no terminal.

You: open the app → Robinhood panel → choose a date → Run import → see how many trades came in.

2 Clean trade reporting — per day / week / month next

See your actual trades grouped by day, week, and month, with the numbers that matter: total P&L, win rate, average win vs. loss, best and worst trade, and your win/loss streak. One view, a simple day / week / month toggle, plus your running P&L curve.

You: open the app → see "this week I'm +$X, 6 wins / 2 losses" instead of scrolling a raw list.

3 Option chains + greeks building

Look up any symbol's option chain right in the app — every strike for an expiration with its delta, theta, gamma, IV, and open interest. For sizing and picking contracts, without leaving the platform.

You: type a symbol + expiration → see the chain with greeks → pick the strike you want.

4 Did the signals actually work? later

Tie your real trades back to the scanner alerts — which signals you acted on, and whether acting on them made money vs. the alert's plan. This is what tells you the platform's real edge, not just guesswork.

You: see "PDL reclaim alerts you traded: 7 taken, 5 winners, +$X" — proof the signals work.

Not doing: live buying & selling from the app. Placing real orders is a different, much riskier thing — real money through an unofficial connection, with rules about trading on people's behalf. We're deliberately staying read-only for now: see your trades, evaluate them, look up options. Execution is a separate decision for later.
